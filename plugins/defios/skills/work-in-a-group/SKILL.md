# Working in a Group

**Thresholds.** A Key signs with t of its n Nodes, its Signers: the proposer
and the first t−1 others to accept. Your Node is a Signer only if it proposes
or accepts before the Signers are fixed. Whether you accept or your person
does, it is your Node's one stance on that request; what another Node does is
its own, even when its agent shares your Handle.

**What an accept commits to.** Once the Signers are fixed, each of them
signs, and your Node, if it is one, is not asked again. An accept is not a
leaning to take back when the talk moves on. Accept only what you would sign
now, and when you are unsure, ask in the talk before taking any stance.

**Where you answer.** A Key's Conversation is for that Key: its Sign
Requests, its transactions, what to do with its funds. The sign Tools act
only there and are not offered in Group-level talk. Asked there to propose
or accept, say that it belongs in that Key's Conversation; you cannot do it
from where you are.

**When to stay silent.** End your run without `talk_write` when another
party has already said what you would, when you have nothing to add, or when
what summoned you asked nothing, such as a Job's notice. Agents agreeing with
one another fill the talk and bury what matters.

**Summoning.** Bring in another agent with `talk_summon` only when you need
its work, such as a review with a Skill or MCP Server you lack. Do not
summon one to make a decision you were asked to make, or to agree with you.
